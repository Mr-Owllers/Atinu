Grammar

the core of any language

gender

as explained in the philosophy section of the homepage, there are 3 genders in Atinú:
  • xert (heart/abstract/root): ∅
     it [humanity], sakan [peace], tomo [housing]
  • oso (animate/alive): -o (-so for vowel-ending words)
     ito [human], sakano [fish], tomoso [home]
  • śuk (inanimate/dead): -uk (-śuk for vowel-ending words)
     ituk [corpse], sakanuk [idiot], tomośuk [house]

plurality

  • singular: ∅
     it [humanity], sakano [fish], tomośuk [house]
  • paucal (a few): -ól (-ló for vowel-ending words)
     itól [some humanity], sakanoló [a few fish], tomośukól [a few houses]
  • plural: -es (-se for vowel-ending words)
     ites [a lot of humanity], sakanose [many fish], tomośukes [many houses]

cases

there are 6 cases:
  • nominative: ∅
     di ito pode [the person walks]
  • dative: a- (aq- for vowels)
     di ito pode ami [the person walks to me]
  • genitive: -no (-yno for consonants)
     di ito pode atomoso mino [the person walks to my home]
    •  NOTE: you could also say "di ito pode atomoso no mi", both work fine
  • dimunitive: -il (-lil for vowels)
     di itilo pode a di tomolilo [the tiny person walks to the tiny house]
  • augmentative: -ák (-bák for vowels)
     di itáko pode a di tomobáko [the big person walks to the big house]
  • vocative: ó! oi! ei!
     ó! ito, údopode atomolilo mino! [hey! person, walk to my tiny house!]

tenses

  • present: ∅
     di ito pode [the person walks]
  • past: -eþ (-éþ if last letter is e)
     di ito podéþ [the person walked]
  • future: couko a
     di ito coukus a pode [the person is going to walk]

aspect

  • perfective: ∅
     di ito pode [the person walks]
  • progressive: -us (omit last vowel)
     di ito podus [the person is walking]
  • habitual: ebo- (eboq- for vowels)
     di ito ebopode ses dia [the person walks everyday]

mood

  • indicative: ∅
     di ito pode [the person walks]
  • imperative: údo- (údon- for vowels)
     údopode atomolilo mino! [walk to my tiny house!]
  • subjunctive: ví- (vís- for vowels)
     di ito vípode [the person wishes to walk]

syntax

  • word order: SVO (subject-verb-object)
     di ito pode ami [the person walks to me]
  • adjectives: A-N
     di itilo pode a di roxot tomolilo mino [the tiny person walks to my tiny red house]
  • adpositions: prepositions
     di ito pode im di tomolilo [the person walks in the tiny house]
  • possessee → possessor
     di tomolilo mino [my tiny house]
  • aux verb → main verb
     di ito coukus a pode ami [the person is going to walk to me]

valency-changing operations

  • causative: duz
     di ito duz pode ami [the person makes me walk]
  • passive: èrhal
     di ito èrhal pode ami [the person is walked by me]
